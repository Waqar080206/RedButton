## 2. Description of the Service

Red Button provides AI-assisted retrieval of equipment safety information from documentation uploaded by your organization's administrators, and optional escalation features that, upon confirmation by an authorized human ("Shift Supervisor"), may place phone calls or send text messages to designated contacts.

The Service is a **decision-support tool**. It is not a certified safety system, not a medical device, and not an emergency dispatch service.

## 3. No substitute for official procedures

The Service supplements, and does not replace, your organization's official safety procedures, training, signage, lockout/tagout protocols, or emergency response plans. In any conflict between guidance from the Service and your organization's official procedures or the judgment of a qualified safety professional, the official procedures and professional judgment control.

## 4. Emergency services disclaimer

**The Service does not contact real emergency services (e.g., 911 or local equivalents) unless explicitly configured to do so by your organization with verified, appropriate numbers.** In test, demo, or trial environments, any "emergency services" feature is connected to designated test numbers only. Your organization is solely responsible for configuring, verifying, and testing any live emergency-dispatch integration before relying on it, and for maintaining independent emergency communication channels (e.g., a direct phone line, physical alarm pull, radio) that do not depend on the Service.

## 5. Accuracy of information

Answers are generated from documentation ("manuals," "SOPs") uploaded by your organization's administrators. The Service does not independently verify the accuracy, completeness, or currency of uploaded documents. Your organization is responsible for ensuring uploaded materials are accurate and up to date. The Service may decline to answer, or may indicate low confidence and escalate to a human, rather than provide a guess — this is by design and should not be interpreted as a system malfunction.

## 6. Human confirmation requirement

Certain features (including but not limited to emergency call initiation, mass worker notification, and backup requests) require explicit confirmation by an authorized Shift Supervisor before any action is taken. Your organization is responsible for ensuring Supervisors are trained, available, and empowered to make these confirmations promptly.

## 7. User roles and responsibilities

- **Workers** must continue to follow site safety training and procedures and use the Service as a supplementary aid only.
- **Shift Supervisors** are responsible for exercising independent judgment when confirming or declining any proposed action; the Service's proposal is a suggestion, not an instruction.
- **Administrators** are responsible for the accuracy of uploaded content, correct equipment tagging, and appropriate management of user accounts and access.
